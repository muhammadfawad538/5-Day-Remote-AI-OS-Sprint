# Senior Backend Engineer

**Company:** Northwind Systems
**Department:** Platform Engineering
**Location:** Remote (US-based)
**Employment Type:** Full-time

---

## About the Role

Northwind Systems is building a high-throughput data pipeline platform used by
fortune 500 clients. We're looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to own core
services, improve reliability, and mentor junior engineers.

---

## Responsibilities

- Design and build RESTful APIs and background job processors in Python.
- Own database schema design and query optimisation for PostgreSQL.
- Participate in on-call rotation and incident response.
- Write and review code, enforce standards, and mentor 2–3 junior engineers.
- Collaborate with product and data teams to translate requirements into
  technical specs.
- Contribute to internal tooling and developer experience improvements.

---

## Must-Have Qualifications

- **5+ years** of professional backend engineering experience.
- Expert-level **Python** (3.10+, type hints, async/await, pytest).
- Production experience with **PostgreSQL** — schema design, indexing, query
  optimisation.
- Experience building and operating **REST APIs** (FastAPI, Flask, or Django).
- Familiarity with **Docker** and containerised deployments.
- Experience writing tests (unit, integration, and some E2E).

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- Experience with message queues (Kafka, RabbitMQ, or AWS SQS).
- Familiarity with AWS services (ECS, RDS, CloudWatch).
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI).
- Contributions to open-source projects.

---

## Education

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field, OR equivalent
  practical experience.
