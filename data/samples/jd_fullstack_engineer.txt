# Senior Full-Stack Engineer

**Company:** Pinnacle Digital
**Department:** Product Engineering
**Location:** Hybrid — New York, NY (3 days/week in office)
**Employment Type:** Full-time

---

## About the Role

Pinnacle Digital is building a next-generation SaaS platform for e-commerce analytics.
We need a Senior Full-Stack Engineer who can own features end-to-end, from React
front-ends to Node.js APIs.

---

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain React front-end components and Node.js REST/GraphQL APIs.
- Own database schema design and optimisation (PostgreSQL).
- Participate in architectural reviews and technical planning.
- Mentor junior engineers through code review and pair programming.
- Collaborate with designers and product managers to ship polished features.
- Contribute to CI/CD pipelines and developer tooling.

---

## Must-Have Qualifications

- **5+ years** of professional full-stack engineering experience.
- Expert-level **React** (hooks, state management, TypeScript).
- Production experience with **Node.js** and Express or similar frameworks.
- Experience with **PostgreSQL** or another relational database in production.
- Familiarity with **TypeScript** and type-safe development practices.
- Experience with **CI/CD** pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or similar).

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- Experience with **GraphQL** (Apollo, Relay, or similar).
- Familiarity with **AWS** (ECS, RDS, S3, CloudFront).
- Experience with **Docker** and containerised deployments.
- Contributions to **open-source** projects.
- Experience with **monitoring/observability** tools (Datadog, New Relic).

---

## Education

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field, OR equivalent
  practical experience.
