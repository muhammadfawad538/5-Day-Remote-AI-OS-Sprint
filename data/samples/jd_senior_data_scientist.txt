# Senior Data Scientist

**Company:** Meridian Health Tech
**Department:** Data & AI
**Location:** Remote (US-based)
**Employment Type:** Full-time

---

## About the Role

Meridian Health Tech is using machine learning to improve patient outcomes and
reduce healthcare costs. We're seeking a Senior Data Scientist to build and
deploy predictive models at scale.

---

## Responsibilities

- Design, train, and evaluate machine learning models for healthcare applications.
- Collaborate with clinical and engineering teams to translate business problems
  into ML solutions.
- Own the end-to-end ML pipeline: data ingestion, feature engineering, model
  training, evaluation, and deployment.
- Write production-quality code (Python) and comprehensive tests.
- Communicate results and model behaviour to non-technical stakeholders.
- Mentor junior data scientists and contribute to our data science best practices.

---

## Must-Have Qualifications

- **Master's degree** in Machine Learning, Statistics, Computer Science, or a
  related quantitative field. (PhD preferred.)
- **5+ years** of industry experience building and deploying ML models.
- Expert-level **Python** for data science (pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn).
- Production experience with **deep learning** frameworks (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Strong understanding of **NLP** and/or **computer vision** techniques.
- Experience with **MLOps** tools (MLflow, Kubeflow, or similar).

## Nice-to-Have Qualifications

- Experience with **healthcare** or **clinical** data (FHIR, EHR).
- Familiarity with **cloud ML platforms** (AWS SageMaker, GCP Vertex AI).
- Published **research** in top-tier ML venues (NeurIPS, ICML, ACL, CVPR).
- Experience with **large language models** and prompt engineering.
- Proficiency in **SQL** and experience with large-scale data warehouses.

---

## Education

- Master's degree in a quantitative field (PhD preferred).
- Equivalent practical experience may substitute for formal education.
